FAQ

Straight answers.

Is this the same as Naka?

No. Naka is the managed front desk — phones, chat, booking, follow-up. Consulting is custom work for the load behind the line. Many teams start with one. Some need both. We will recommend one first move.

Do I need an AI employee to work with you?

No. Bring the bottleneck. If a custom teammate is wrong and a front desk is right, we will say so. If neither is right, we will say that too.

Is this only for schools?

Education is where we are especially strong. Lean operators in other fields are welcome when the problem is the same shape: too much repeatable work on too few people.

Will you replace teachers or counselors?

No. That is not the offer. People keep the human work. We take the grind that keeps them from it.

How do we start?

Book 30 minutes. Come with the thing that is stealing the week. We will be straight about fit, scope, and timing.
